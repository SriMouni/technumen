Our Security & Compliance Heritage

Built on a foundation of global best practices, rigorous audits, and continuous security improvement.

🏆

Audit Heritage

Proudly leveraging global best practices with our CMMi – SVC Level 3 appraisal, ISO/IEC 27001:2013 certification (currently upgrading to the 2022 standard), and regular SOC 2 Type II audits. Our process maturity is independently verified and continuously maintained.

🛡

Infrastructure Defense

Protecting networks via Microsoft Azure security services, O365 infrastructure, and advanced cloud-native Endpoint Detection and Threat Response (EDTR) platforms. Third-party security experts also perform broad annual penetration tests.

🔐

Identity & Access Control

Enforcing Conditional Access Policies based on user/device trustworthiness and Multi-factor Authentication (MFA) to prevent identity breaches. Zero-trust principles govern every access decision.

📜

Operational Security

Strict adherence to security awareness training, incident response protocols, routine phishing campaigns, and comprehensive background checks (criminal, education, and employment) for all hires.
