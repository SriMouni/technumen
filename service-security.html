Security Services Portfolio

With cyber-threats evolving in scale and sophistication, organizations need a trusted security partner. Technumen delivers enterprise-grade cybersecurity services tailored to your risk profile, compliance requirements, and business objectives.

Key OfferingsSecurity Capabilities

01

Cloud Security Services

Protect your cloud infrastructure with our comprehensive cloud security services. We provide cloud security architecture, configuration audits, workload protection, and continuous monitoring across AWS, Azure, and GCP environments.

02

Product Security Services

Embed security into your software development lifecycle. Our product security services include secure SDLC consulting, application security testing (SAST, DAST, SCA), threat modeling, and DevSecOps integration.

03

GRC Services

Governance, Risk and Compliance services to help you meet regulatory requirements and manage security risks effectively. We provide risk assessments, compliance audits, policy development, and security awareness training.

04

IAM Services

Identity and Access Management solutions to secure user access across your enterprise. We implement SSO, MFA, privileged access management, identity governance, and zero trust access controls.

05

MDR Services

Managed Detection and Response services providing 24/7 threat monitoring, incident response, and threat hunting capabilities. Our SOC analysts and advanced SIEM solutions ensure rapid detection and containment of security threats.
